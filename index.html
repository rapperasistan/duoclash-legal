Before you ask

Questions

Do we both need the app?

Yes. Each of you installs Duo Clash on your own iPhone, makes a profile, and has your own subscription. Then one of you shares a code and you're paired.

What does it cost?

Duo Clash is a weekly subscription with a lower price for the first week. The exact price for your country is shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Does it work if we're in different countries?

Yes. Calls work on Wi-Fi and mobile data anywhere. On very strict networks the call is passed through a relay, still encrypted, so it still connects.

Which languages can we play in?

The app speaks 16 languages. Word games can be played in 12 of them, and every game without words works in any of them. You each pick your own app language.

Is it only for couples?

Not at all. It's made for any two people: partners, best friends, siblings, a long-distance roommate. It just only ever has two seats.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Duo Clash is on iPhone for now.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings → Delete account removes your name, picture, pairing and match history for good. Unpairing removes your shared scoreboard for both of you.
