Who wears the crown?

Every win puts points on your weekly board. The leader takes the crown, then it all starts over.

  • A weekly board just for the two of you
  • Every match and score in your history
  • One more game: same, another, or random

Private by design

Our server only introduces your two phones. Video and voice go straight between them, encrypted.

  • Calls are never recorded or stored
  • No ads, no trackers, no email needed
  • Report, block or unpair in one tap

Questions

Anything else? support@duoclash.app

Do we both need the app?

Yes. Each of you installs Duo Clash on your own iPhone and has your own subscription. One of you shares a code and you're paired.

What does it cost?

A weekly subscription with a lower price for the first week. Your country's price is shown in the app before you subscribe; cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Does it work across countries?

Yes, on Wi-Fi or mobile data anywhere. On very strict networks the call goes through a relay, still encrypted, so it still connects.

Which languages can we play in?

The app speaks 16 languages. Word games are played in 12 of them; every game without words works in all of them.

Is it only for couples?

No. Partners, best friends, siblings: any two people. It just only ever has two seats.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Duo Clash is on iPhone for now.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Settings → Delete account removes your name, picture, pairing and match history for good.

Can I suggest a game?

Please do. Write to support@duoclash.app; new games are added all the time.
