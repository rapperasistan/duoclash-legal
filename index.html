Three taps to game night

No usernames to hunt down, no links to send around. Just the two of you.

1

Pair once

One of you shares a 6-digit code, the other types it in. That's it, you're a duo.

2

Start a call

Tap Call and your duo gets a notification. You see each other right away.

3

Pick a game

Both cameras stay on at the top of every game, so you see every reaction.

2 player games, face to face

Word games, number games, memory and speed, strategy. From chess and four in a row to Guess the Celebrity.

🎥

Your faces in every game

The video call lives inside the games, not in another app. Mic, camera and hang up are one tap away.

👑

A weekly scoreboard

Every win counts. The week's champion takes the crown, and the scores start over every week.

💞

Just the two of you

Made for exactly two: a couple across the world, or best friends across town.

🔒

Private by design

Calls are encrypted from phone to phone and never recorded. No ads, no tracking.
